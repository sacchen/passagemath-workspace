Evidence for the plain-kernel claim in pr-body.md.

plain-kernel-gyroid-REPRODUCER.ipynb carries NO outputs. It is the input, not
the record: the executed notebook is 10.9 MB, almost all of it one escaped
Three.js document, and is not worth storing. Regenerate it with

  jupyter nbconvert --to notebook --execute \
      --ExecutePreprocessor.timeout=600 \
      --output executed.ipynb plain-kernel-gyroid-REPRODUCER.ipynb

run from a venv holding the patched build (see redteam-prompt.md). The
generator that wrote the notebook is make-plain-kernel-notebook.py.

Measured on 2026-08-28, .venv-plot3d, patched build:

  cell 0 stdout   kernel formatter: IPython.core.formatters.DisplayFormatter
  cell 1 bundle   ['image/png', 'text/html', 'text/plain']
                  image/png     128596 chars
                  text/html   10767295 chars
                  text/plain        17 chars

plain-kernel-notebook.png is that executed notebook rendered by
`jupyter nbconvert --to html` and screenshotted, showing the scene drawn from
the text/html output.

Sandbox test, same day, same build: an iframe carrying the sphere page with
sandbox="allow-scripts" and one without produced byte-identical screenshots
(sha256 60d1645e2463b616...). The threejs assets in src/sage/ext_data/threejs
touch no localStorage, sessionStorage, cookie, parent, top, or postMessage API.
